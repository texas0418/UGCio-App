UGCio

Privacy policy & support

The short version: UGCio keeps your data on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of ours. The only data that ever leaves your phone is what you send yourself — invoices and payment reminders through apps you choose, backups you export, and purchases processed by the app store.

What UGCio stores

Your portfolio, rates, deals, and settings are stored in a local database on your device. We cannot see, access, or recover this data — it never reaches us. Deleting the app deletes the data, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Sharing your portfolio

When you share your portfolio or rate card, UGCio hands the content to the app you choose — sending happens from your own accounts under your control.

Purchases

UGCio Pro is an auto-renewing subscription purchased through the app store on your device (Apple App Store or Google Play). Purchases are processed by the store and validated by RevenueCat, our purchase-management provider, using an anonymous identifier and your store receipt. This does not include your name, email address, students, or any content from the app.

Backups

Backups are plain files you export and keep wherever you like. They are yours; nothing is uploaded anywhere by the app.
